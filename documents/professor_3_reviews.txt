They described the course as easy after the instructor gave an additional drop. They said students could drop their last two quizzes and finish the course before the last two weeks of the semester.

Overall takeaway:
Student reviews describe Michael Nowak's CS 128 course as difficult, workload-heavy, and often frustrating. Common complaints include long lessons, lecture-heavy structure, wordy documentation, confusing lesson activities, busy work, and quizzes or tests that feel harsh because of limited or no partial credit. However, one visible review says the course became easy after an additional quiz drop policy. Overall, the visible reviews suggest that students should expect a high workload, structured online lessons, mandatory attendance in some offerings, and a need for significant independent effort.
